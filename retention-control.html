The case study

Where retention is won or lost.

At Tobipets, I engineered the automated communication workflows and lifecycle funnels that lifted customer loyalty by 15% — the same discipline this case study applies to N26's engagement and retention CRM, month two onwards.

Disclaimer This is an independent strategic exercise based on publicly available information and illustrative datasets. It does not use confidential N26 data.

Why month two?

The first month is onboarding's job — set up the account, first transaction, first habit. What happens after that determines whether a customer becomes a long-term user or a quiet churn statistic. That's the segment this role owns, and where CRM work is hardest to get right.

The core problem

Retention isn't one campaign — it's a system that has to catch three different moments: the customer settling into a habit, the customer drifting, and the customer who's already gone quiet. Each needs a different message, trigger and channel.
